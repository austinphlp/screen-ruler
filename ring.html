Ring size

Lay a ring that fits you flat on the circle. Adjust until the circle's outer edge just touches the inside of the ring.

  1. Wrap a thin strip of paper around the base of your finger, snug but not tight.
  2. Mark the point where the paper overlaps itself.
  3. Measure from the end of the strip to your mark.
